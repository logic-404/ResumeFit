Senior ML Engineer — Atlassian, AI Platform team

About the role
We're looking for a Senior ML Engineer to join Atlassian's AI Platform team. You'll design and ship retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems that power features used by millions of teams worldwide. You'll own production ML pipelines end-to-end: data ingestion, model serving, evaluation, and observability.

What you'll do
- Build and operate RAG pipelines on production-grade infrastructure
- Design vector-search and reranking strategies that scale
- Partner with product engineers to ship LLM features
- Drive MLOps practices: experiment tracking, model registry, CI/CD for ML

Required
- 4+ years of professional software engineering experience, with at least 2 in ML
- Strong Python; experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Direct experience building RAG systems or retrieval pipelines
- Familiarity with cloud (AWS, GCP, or Azure)

Nice to have
- LangChain / LangGraph experience
- Kubernetes
- MLflow or similar experiment tracking
- Prior work on agentic systems

Benefits
- Equity, remote-flexible, learning budget
